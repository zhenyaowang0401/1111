How it works

Set a timer and start digging. Pause to stop the timer and digging. Complete a session to save depth and collect stars.

Each five minutes earns one star (minimum one). A 15-second demo earns one star and five metres. Ending early discards the current dig rewards.

Pet your mole while idle. Equip multiple items together. Reports record actual Study time, including unfinished sessions, but exclude demos and breaks.
