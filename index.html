How it works

Study or work while your mole digs. Every five minutes earns a star; every ten minutes brings a find. Pickups are automatic. Pausing stops progress. Ending early keeps what you earned.

Developer demo: 15 seconds, one star, five metres and one find per completed run. Demo rewards are spendable but excluded from focus reports.

Pet your mole while idle; tap three times quickly for a happy jump and spin. Pause or end your session to drag through the mine. Golden finds have a 3% bonus chance per ten-minute discovery, excluding demos; keep, display at home or sell for 99 stars. Display finds on your mole’s head at home. Mix hats, clothes and scarves; tools appear only while digging. Music: soft music, groove and white noise, supplied by the creator.
